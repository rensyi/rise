Rise, in your browser

An interactive preview of Rise, a wake-up tracker for Android and iPhone. The phone below works: tap the sun, the bottom bar, a calendar day and the settings. It is a mock-up drawn for the web, so sizes and timing differ a little from the real app.

Your device asks for reduced motion, so the movement is switched off on this page. The app switches it off in the same way.

Things to try

  • Log a wake-up: tap the sun on Today. The message that appears has an Undo button.
  • Bedtime: tap Bedtime on Today. The button lets off Zs while it waits.
  • The month: open Calendar. Each day is coloured by how close it was to the target. Tap a day to change or delete it.
  • Consistency: the card under the three numbers on Calendar.
  • Streak: the third tab on Calendar, with three choices for how it counts.
  • Settings: targets, weekend targets, the reminder, time format, theme and backup.

Change the theme from Settings inside the phone; the widgets below follow it. The data here is made up, and it resets when the page reloads.
